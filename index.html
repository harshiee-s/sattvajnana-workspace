Dates you pause here won't count as scheduled sessions for this client — they'll show as No Work in every assigned staff member's attendance (regular and supplementary alike).

Reduce Session Hours

Cut a session short instead of cancelling it. Pick the date and how many hours are actually needed — the assigned staff member's attendance for that date is then measured against the reduced time, not the usual scheduled hours.
